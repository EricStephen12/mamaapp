Account & Data Deletion

Permanently delete your Mama Dish account and all associated personal data—either right here on the web or directly inside the mobile app.

Option 1: Delete Instantly on the Web

If you have already uninstalled the Mama Dish app, verify your account credentials below to immediately and permanently erase your account and all synced data from our servers.

This action is immediate and irreversible. All saved recipes, pantry items, and taste preferences tied to this account will be permanently erased.

Option 2: Delete Directly Inside the Mama Dish App

  1. Open the Mama Dish app on your phone.
  2. Tap your Profile button (or Mama medallion) in the top bar to open your Kitchen Pass.
  3. Select the Account tab.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm Delete Permanently.

Option 3: Forgot Password / Email Deletion Request

If you no longer remember your password and cannot access password reset, email support@mamadish.app from the email address registered to your Mama Dish account with the subject line “Delete My Account”. We process verified email deletion requests within 7 days.

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

Data Type Action Upon Deletion Retention Period
Account Profile (Name, email address, password hash) Permanently deleted from our database 0 days (Immediate)
Kitchen Pass & Taste Profile (Country, budget, dietary restrictions, wellness goals) Permanently deleted via cascading deletion 0 days (Immediate)
Pantry Items, Saved Meals & Roll History Permanently deleted via cascading deletion 0 days (Immediate)
App Store / Google Play Purchase Receipts Managed directly by Google Play / Apple App Store (de-linked from deleted account email) Retained by Google/Apple per statutory tax & financial regulations

Active Subscriptions Reminder: Deleting your Mama Dish account erases your app data, but if you have an active Mama Dish Pro subscription on Google Play, remember to also cancel the recurring subscription under Google Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
