Privacy Policy

How Mama Dish (“Mama”) collects, uses, protects, and lets you control your information.

1. Summary & Our Privacy Promise

Mama Dish is an everyday home-cooking companion that helps you decide what to cook based on your cupboard staples, local grocery prices, available kitchen gear, and dietary preferences. We believe your kitchen habits belong to you:

  • Guest Mode Available: You can use Mama Dish’s core meal recommendations, pantry matcher, and Discover feed without creating an account.
  • Zero Ad Tracking or Data Selling: We do not sell your personal data, rent your email list, or share your data with third-party advertising networks.
  • Encrypted in Transit & Securely Stored: All communication between the mobile app and our backend servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption, and account passwords are one-way hashed using industry-standard bcrypt.
  • Full Deletion Control: If you create an account, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time inside the app or via our Web Account Deletion Portal.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on whether you use Mama Dish as a guest or create an optional account, we handle the following categories of information:

Category Specific Data Purpose & Optionality
Account Information Display name (optional), email address, encrypted password hash Optional (only if you sign up). Used for authentication, password recovery, and syncing your kitchen across devices.
Culinary & Wellness Preferences Selected country & currency, weekly food budget, cooking gear (e.g., 1 Pan), cook-time limit, dietary restrictions (e.g., Halal, Vegan, Gluten-Free, allergies), general nutritional goal (e.g., Balanced Energy, High Protein, Lean & Light) Stored locally on your device in Guest Mode, or synced to your account if signed in, strictly to tailor meal suggestions and local cost estimates.
Kitchen & App Activity Selected pantry ingredients, saved collection recipes, and cooked/skipped meal history Used to suggest recipes matching ingredients you already have at home and avoid repeating skipped dishes.
Subscriptions & Purchases Anonymous entitlement status and purchase receipts for Mama Dish Pro Processed by Google Play Billing / Apple App Store and RevenueCat to unlock Pro features. We never access or store your credit/debit card numbers.

3. Device Permissions & Sensors

  • Motion / Accelerometer Sensor (Local Only): Mama Dish uses your phone’s accelerometer locally in memory solely to detect the optional “Shake to Roll” gesture on the Home screen (which you can toggle off anytime in your Kitchen Pass). Raw motion data never leaves your device and is never stored.
  • No Invasive Permissions: Mama Dish does not access your contacts, microphone, SMS messages, call logs, or background GPS location. Selecting a country in your Kitchen Pass is manual and user-controlled.

4. How AI Meal Recommendations Work

When Mama generates personalized meal ideas, Discover decks, or pantry recipes, the app sends de-identified culinary context (such as your selected country, cooking gear, dietary restrictions, and chosen pantry ingredients—never your email address or password) over HTTPS to our secure backend proxy, which routes culinary prompts through vetted language-model inference providers (via OpenRouter). Prompts are used solely to return your meal suggestions in real time and are not used to build third-party advertising profiles.

5. How We Share Information (Service Providers Only)

We share data strictly with essential infrastructure processors operating on our behalf under confidentiality and security obligations:

  • Cloud Hosting & Database: Render (API hosting) and Neon PostgreSQL (encrypted cloud database storage for registered accounts).
  • AI Inference: OpenRouter (processing de-identified recipe and culinary text prompts).
  • Subscription Management: Google Play Billing, Apple App Store, and RevenueCat (verifying Mama Dish Pro subscription entitlements).
  • Transactional Emails: Resend (delivering account welcome and password-reset verification codes—zero third-party marketing spam).

We may also disclose information if strictly required by applicable law, valid court order, or to protect the safety and security of our users and infrastructure.

6. Data Security

  • All network requests between the Mama Dish app and our servers are encrypted in transit via HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text; they are salted and hashed using bcrypt (cost factor 12) with constant-time verification.
  • Session tokens and local meal caches on your mobile device are stored using platform-protected storage (expo-secure-store / Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).

7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Instant Permanent Deletion: You can delete your Mama Dish account and all associated database records at any time—either inside the app (Profile → Account → Delete Account) or online at our Account & Data Deletion Portal.

When you delete your account, your User record, email address, password hash, TasteProfile, PantryItems, SavedMeals, and MealDecisions are immediately and permanently deleted from our active database via cascading deletion. If you use Mama Dish without an account, uninstalling the mobile app removes all locally stored preferences from your device.

8. Health & Nutritional Wellness Notice

Mama Dish provides everyday home-cooking inspiration, estimated ingredient costs, user-selected dietary filters, and general wellness/nutrition categories (such as Clean & Healthy, High Protein, and Light). Mama Dish is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, clinical nutrition therapy, or disease diagnosis. Always verify physical food packaging for allergens and consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical dietary needs.

9. Children’s Privacy

Mama Dish is designed for general audiences aged 13 and older who plan or cook meals and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us at support@mamadish.app and we will promptly delete the account.

10. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA, NDPA)

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • Access and review the personal data associated with your account;
  • Update or correct your profile, country, dietary rules, and pantry items directly inside the app;
  • Permanently erase your account and personal data at any time;
  • Withdraw consent or object to processing where applicable.

11. Changes to This Policy & Contact Us

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we improve Mama Dish. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the top of this page.
